How It Works

From Conversation to Live Product

The same process for a business website or a full management system — just deeper on discovery and testing for the latter.

01

Discover

A conversation — via WhatsApp or call — about your business or organisation, your goals, and what success looks like. For a system, this includes mapping the actual workflow it needs to support.

02

Design

A layout and structure aligned with your brand and your users — you see a preview before anything is built.

03

Build

Clean, mobile-responsive, SEO-ready code for a website — or the working screens, data structure, and logic for a system. You're updated throughout.

04

Launch

Your site or system goes live. I walk you through everything, hand over files and credentials, and stay available for support afterward.
